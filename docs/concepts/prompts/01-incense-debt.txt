Create an original, polished art-direction concept for a 2D top-down action roguelite game called Incense Debt. Landscape game concept keyframe, 1536 x 1024 composition. Do not put any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, HUD, or title into the image.

PLAYER FANTASY: a tiny paper-bodied shrine apprentice burns offerings to fight living folk-monster debts inside an abandoned temple. The paper apprentice wears a short vermilion robe, an ivory paper mask with two simple black eyes, and carries a small bronze incense burner. Cute but eerie, memorable and readable at small game-sprite scale.

COMPOSITION: occupy most of the canvas with one fully visible enclosed combat room, seen from a fixed near-overhead orthographic game camera, floor viewed from above, characters drawn with visible expressive faces as flat game sprites. Clear rectangular walkable floor and four doorways. Place the hero near the lower center, three large readable enemy silhouettes around the middle and upper sides, one mini-boss near the upper right. Keep ample empty floor between figures. Show active gameplay rather than a scenic environment painting. At the lower right, reserve a small unobtrusive inset with the same hero enlarged on a plain warm-paper background and two compact pickup icons, clearly separated from the combat arena.

SIGNATURE ACTION: two red paper-lantern ghosts and a folded-paper hound are linked by a curling orange-gold incense trail. One linked enemy is bursting into a crisp circle of glowing ash and angular paper fragments. A small handful of blue-green enemy bullets approach the hero with obvious clear gaps. Projectiles, character silhouettes, pickups and the walkable floor must remain distinct; never fill the screen with sparks.

ART DIRECTION: original Chinese folk woodblock prints combined with layered paper-cut shapes, chunky brush-ink outlines, ragged fiber-paper edges, flat graphic shading, restrained handmade texture. Use a stylized vermilion / soot-black / ivory / aged-gold palette, plus very limited jade-cyan for enemy projectiles. No pixel art. No realistic 3D rendering. No generic anime. No religious calligraphy. No borrowed videogame characters. Avoid ultra-detailed architecture, oversaturated bloom and gradients. Every visual element should look adaptable into small hand-drawn sprites and modular room tiles.

ROOM: a faded jade-gray stone floor with subtle paper-like grain, torn vermilion shrine curtains around the outside walls, two breakable folded-paper offering stacks at the sides, and a shrine doorway at the top. Lighting is graphic and even, shadows are short. The image should feel simultaneously mischievous, haunted, tactile and punchy, with very strong gameplay readability.
