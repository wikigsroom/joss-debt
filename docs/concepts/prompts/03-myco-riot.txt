Create an original, polished art-direction concept for a 2D top-down action roguelite game called Myco Riot. Landscape game concept keyframe, 1536 x 1024 composition. Do not put any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, HUD, or title into the image.

PLAYER FANTASY: a little mushroom scavenger has bonded with an unstable living spore-core. The hero has a large coral-red mushroom cap, a pale cream rounded body, dark simple eyes, tiny charcoal boots, and a visibly mutated seed-shooting arm. Cute strange biology rather than horror gore. Design a silhouette that works at small sprite scale.

COMPOSITION: most of the canvas is a complete enclosed organic greenhouse combat room, fixed near-overhead orthographic 2D game camera; floor seen from above, flat expressive game sprites with visible faces. Four root-framed doorways, open walkable floor. Hero at lower center, a spotted seed-tick at left, a droplet-shaped fungal leaper at upper middle, a hollow rotten fruit brute at right, and a large carnivorous mushroom bud near upper right. Keep clean open movement corridors. At lower right, a small unobtrusive inset displays the same hero enlarged on pale cream, plus two distinctive organic mutation pickups.

SIGNATURE ACTION: three luminous mint mycelium nodes, no more than three, lie in a loose curve on the floor, connected with thin subtle root filaments. The mushroom hero fires a chunky branching cluster of seed projectiles. One killed enemy is sprouting a new coral mushroom node. A wave of amber-green growth visibly pulses out from one node toward two enemies, suggesting a chain bloom. A few lavender hostile spores have clear recognizable outlines, never visually confused with friendly seeds.

ART DIRECTION: handmade illustrated field-biology storybook with chunky contour lines, gouache blocks, cut-paper plants, soft imperfections, sharp simple silhouettes. Colors: pale desaturated sage floor, charcoal-violet shadows, coral cap, mint bioluminescent friendly growth, ochre soil, lavender hostile projectiles. Limit the glowing effect to projectiles and node edges. No pixel art, no realistic 3D, no gore, no generic fantasy elf, no anime, no borrowed videogame characters. Scalable assets, bold shapes and modular root walls.

ROOM: greenhouse tiles partly overtaken by thick root walls, restrained low-detail leaf shapes at the perimeter, broken ceramic planters along the sides. Flat diffuse lighting with short compact shadows. The scene should convey satisfying organic mutation, interconnected growth and slightly mischievous ecology while keeping combat extremely legible.
